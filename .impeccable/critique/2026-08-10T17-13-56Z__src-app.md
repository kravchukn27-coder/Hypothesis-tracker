## Design Specificity Verdict

The UI is a coherent, restrained Operate tool, but its visual language is still category-interchangeable: a neutral monochrome admin shell rather than an interface that visibly centres the chain “hypothesis → experiment → weekly plan”. The code expresses the relationship well; the hierarchy does not always surface it.

The deterministic scan reported three warnings: `side-tab` at `src/app/backlog/page.tsx:211` and `src/app/experiments/page.tsx:283` for status-side borders, plus `overused-font` at `src/app/globals.css:25` for Arial. The two side borders are plausible intentional status affordances and should be treated as false positives unless visual review proves them decorative. Arial is a valid neutral fallback but contributes little product character.

## Overall Impression

The product has a strong functional substrate for structured hypothesis work. Its greatest opportunity is to make the next decision unmistakable in every view, especially in the calendar where interaction power currently exceeds discoverability.

## What's Working

- The neutral zinc palette, borders, and restrained CTA treatment suit a dense internal tool.
- Entity connections are thoughtfully implemented across hypothesis, experiment, and calendar contexts.
- Tables share widths and fixed layout, use tabular numbers for Score, show empty values as an em dash, and provide filtering, sorting, and bulk actions.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Tables hide decision-making context

Names/comments truncate and fixed 164px table surfaces turn key lists into small scroll panes. Let tables grow to several rows or use a taller viewport with sticky headers; add an explicit “next action / experiments” text signal; provide accessible expansion or a visible tooltip for truncated values. Suggested command: `$impeccable shape «рабочая таблица backlog и experiments»`, then `$impeccable layout`.

### [P1] Calendar editing has hidden complexity

A cell can assign a stage, open a menu, drag, resize through a narrow edge, and delete on hover. Introduce a visible edit mode or explicit selected-week actions, concise gesture guidance, preview and undo for schedule changes, and a non-hover deletion route. Suggested command: `$impeccable shape «понятное редактирование календарного плана»`, then `$impeccable harden`.

### [P1] Status terminology is not unified

Russian workflow copy is mixed with `Backlog`, `Experiments`, `Calendar`, `Name`, `Status`, `Score`, and `Comment`. Adopt a single glossary and use it across tables, cards, and calendar; explain the current status and allowed next transition in the hypothesis card. Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`.

### [P2] Hypothesis cards show fields, not the decision story

Status, Score, Funnel Level, experiments, result, and next action are distributed across sections. Put a read-only summary at the top with those facts and one context-sensitive CTA; keep detailed editing fields below. Suggested command: `$impeccable shape «summary и next action карточки гипотезы»`.

### [P2] Responsive and accessibility resilience need verification

At `<640px`, column widths are released while the outer table container disables horizontal scrolling. Seven-column tables may become unreadable. Define a compact mobile representation or reduce visible columns with details disclosure; test focus, 200% zoom, contrast, and all no-hover actions. Suggested commands: `$impeccable adapt`, then `$impeccable audit`.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Alex, power user:** no visible shortcuts, saved views, text search, or bulk status change; working through a large set will require repeated filter changes and card opens.
- **Jordan, first-timer:** score scales, status semantics, and the hypothesis-to-experiment-to-week plan are not taught in the first task; the calendar does not reveal its editable gestures.
- **Sam, accessibility-dependent:** hover-only calendar deletion and narrow resize handles lack an obvious keyboard-equivalent path; cramped narrow-table behaviour risks loss of meaning at zoom.

## Minor Observations

- Truncated dates have `title` support but need a discoverable, keyboard-accessible full-value route.
- Navigation active state could use a discreet indicator, not only weight/color.
- `aria-disabled` on “Сегодня” does not make a link semantically disabled.
- The empty calendar offers creating an experiment directly while the rest of the workflow creates it from a hypothesis; clarify that path.

## Questions to Consider

- What must be clear about a hypothesis in five seconds: potential, current validation, or next action?
- Is the calendar primarily an overview or an expert editor? If both, should those modes be separated?
- What is the minimum table column set that enables a decision without opening a card?
